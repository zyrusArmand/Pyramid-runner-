PYRAMID STACKER is a build-and-climb arcade game: raise a pyramid stone by stone, then race up the very one you built — dodging traps and chasing the capstone.
Levels grow bigger and tougher as you climb, with new mechanics introduced one at a time. Your progress, coins and best runs are saved, and top scores post to a worldwide leaderboard.
Crafted with Three.js · WebGL. Built with care for everyone who has the link. Thanks for playing! 💛
